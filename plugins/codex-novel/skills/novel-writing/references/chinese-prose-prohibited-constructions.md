# Chinese Prose Prohibited Constructions

Treat this as the author's maintained constraint list for Codex-authored Chinese prose. Apply it
while drafting and while reviewing every exact Draft revision. A listed construction is a prose
defect even when the surrounding passage is otherwise clear or project-specific.

Check grammatical structure and rhetorical function, not raw substrings. A standalone `只`, `只是`,
`只有`, or `没有` is not automatically a violation. Do not silently alter an exact approved source
quotation or author-supplied quotation; identify it as quoted material and ask before changing it.

## 1. Sentence-opening `……的时候，……`

Do not open a sentence or paragraph with a temporal frame ending in `的时候` before the main
action, including `当……的时候，……` and `在……的时候，……`.

Rejected:

> 太阳落山的时候，他们来到了山脚下。

Begin with the actor, action, change, or immediately perceived state. Keep time only when it is
material, and express it through the concrete state or simultaneous action instead of preserving
the same shell elsewhere in the sentence.

Possible structural direction, not a replacement template:

> 他们赶到山脚，最后一线日光正从山脊退去。

Do not "fix" the example by writing `他们在太阳落山的时候来到了山脚下`; that only relocates the
same construction.

## 2. Paired `没有……只……` contrast

Do not state a contrast through either of these paired constructions, regardless of punctuation:

- `没有 A，只/只是/只有 B`;
- `只/只是/只有 B，没有 A`.

Rejected:

> 屋里没有灯，只有窗外的月光。
>
> 他只是低头赶路，没有回头。

State the material image, action, or condition directly. If the absence of A matters, make its
specific consequence perceptible without rebuilding the same binary assertion in reverse order.

Possible structural directions, not replacement templates:

> 窗外的月光铺进屋里。
>
> 他低头赶路，始终背对着她。

Do not evade the constraint by swapping the two halves, splitting them into adjacent sentences, or
changing `只` to `只是` or `只有`.

## Draft and Review pass

Before saving a Draft and before recommending `ready`:

1. Inspect sentence and paragraph openings for a `的时候` frame that delays the main action.
2. Inspect each sentence and adjacent rhetorical unit for both orders of the paired
   `没有` + `只/只是/只有` contrast.
3. Rebuild each violation from the scene's actual actor, action, perception, state, or consequence;
   do not perform a word-level synonym swap.
4. Re-read the reconstructed passage for causality, meaning, rhythm, and project-specific voice.

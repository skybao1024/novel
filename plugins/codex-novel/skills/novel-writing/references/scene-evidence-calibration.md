# Chinese Scene-Evidence Calibration

Read all three examples before drafting the first Chinese prose candidate or performing the first
Chinese prose Review in the current task. The labels and passages were supplied by the author as a
contrast set. They calibrate structural literary judgment; they are not detector ground truth for
unrelated prose.

Explicit author instructions, approved Writing Rules, project-specific positive anchors, exact
approved manuscript voice, continuity, and viewpoint remain authoritative. Use this reference to
avoid generic rhetorical expansion, not to make every Novel imitate these sentences, objects,
period, emotional register, or narrative distance.

## Example 1 — reject: confirmed AI

> 我与父亲不相见已二年余了，我最不能忘记的是他的背影。这二年的光阴，说长不长，仿佛只是几个寒暑的交替；说短却也不短，足足有七百多个日夜，足以让院角的梅树抽发两回新枝，也足以让许多记忆，像旧宣纸上的墨迹，渐渐晕开，变得模糊。然而，独独那个背影，却像用刀子深深地镌刻在我心版上的一般，非但没有随着时日淡去，反倒愈发清晰、愈发沉重起来。
>
> 那是几年前一个冬天的下午。我那时正要离家北上，继续我那半是求学、半是漂泊的生涯。天色是阴阴的，铅灰色的云，沉沉地压着屋檐，像要掉下来似的。院子里几株枯秃的槐树，枝丫戟张地指向天空，更添了几分萧索。父亲本说是不送我的，他店里事忙，年里关账，琐碎得紧。我也再三劝他不必劳步，我一个二十出头的人，难道还照管不了自己么？他只是“唔唔”地应着，踱到窗边，望着外边，半晌不语。
>
> 及至我提着行李，将要走出大门时，却听见后面熟悉的、略带些蹒跚的脚步声。回头一看，父亲正走了来。他已穿上了那件我极熟识的、洗得有些发白的青布棉袍，只是身子似乎更佝偻了些，像是被那无形的担子压得更弯了。
>
> “我送你到门口，叫辆熟识的车子。”他淡淡地说，顺手便来提我那只不算沉的皮箱。我本要自己来，但看他那不容置喙的神色，话到嘴边，又咽了回去。路上，他只是默默地走在我前头半步。

Why it fails as a target pattern:

- It expands an already clear two-year fact through balanced “long/not long, short/not short”
  exposition, day counting, seasonal comparison, and multiple memory metaphors before the scene
  earns that space.
- Stock weather, bare trees, “漂泊”, and an invisible burden arrive as ready-made emotional
  signals. Much of this material could move to another farewell scene unchanged.
- Narration repeatedly announces the meaning of the father's back before concrete present action
  has allowed the reader to experience it.
- The problem is detachable rhetorical completion, not the mere presence of long sentences,
  weather, reflection, or figurative language.

## Example 2 — reject or rebuild: suspected AI

> 我望着他的背影，心里忽然涌起一股说不出的酸楚。曾几何时，这个背影在我眼中是何等的高大、何等的可以倚仗。幼时我骑在他的肩头，觉得他能摘到天上的星子；牵着他的手走路，仿佛走到天涯海角也不必惧怕。可如今，这背影竟显得这样瘦削，这样落寞了。棉袍的下摆，随着他的步子，空空地晃荡着。
>
> 到了车站，他给我拣定了靠窗的一张椅子。我将他给我预备的紫毛大衣铺好座位，他便又嘱我路上小心，夜里要警醒些，不要受凉。这些寻常的话，他翻来覆去地说着，我那时真是过分聪明了，心里总觉他不大漂亮，有些啰嗦。现在想来，那时真是太不知事了。
>
> 他嘱我等着，自己蹒跚地穿过铁道，要到那边栅栏外去给我买几个橘子。

Why it remains suspect as a target pattern:

- It contains concrete continuity and objects, but the emotion is first labeled and then enlarged
  through a generic childhood montage: shoulder riding, reaching stars, fearless travel, former
  greatness, and present decline.
- The montage supplies sentimental history instead of letting the current encounter reveal the
  relationship. Use memory only when approved history or a present trigger makes it specific and
  changes the viewpoint's current response.
- The physical detail of the empty hem is useful, but surrounding declarations interpret it in
  advance. Retain the strongest specific evidence and rebuild the emotional movement around what
  the viewpoint actually observes, does, withholds, or misreads.
- “Suspected AI” is the author's calibration label, not a universal verdict produced by a phrase
  list.

## Example 3 — positive calibration: human-authored

> 那边月台上，卖东西的少，也须得走下月台，穿过铁道，再跳下去爬上那边月台，这于他，本不是一件容易的事。我本来要去的，他不肯，只好让他去。
>
> 我看见他戴着黑布小帽，穿着黑布大马褂，深青布棉袍，蹒跚地走到铁道边，慢慢探身下去，尚不大难。可是他穿过铁道，要爬上那边月台，就不容易了。他用两手攀着上面，两脚再向上缩；他肥胖的身子向左微倾，显出努力的样子。这时我看见他的背影，我的泪很快地流下来了。我赶紧拭干了泪，怕他看见，也怕别人看见。等他抱着朱红的橘子往回走时，过铁道时，他先将橘子散放在地上，自己慢慢爬下，再抱起橘子走。到这边时，我赶紧去搀他。他和我走到车上，将橘子一股脑儿放在我的皮大衣上。

Why it works as positive structural evidence:

- The emotion accumulates through an exact spatial problem and a visible action sequence: descend,
  cross, climb, brace, shrink the feet upward, set the oranges down, descend again, retrieve them,
  and accept help.
- Ordinary objects and physical facts are not decorative inventory; the platforms, railway,
  clothing, body weight, oranges, and coat determine what can happen and what the viewpoint notices.
- Repeated plain verbs such as walking, climbing, holding, and moving slowly remain because the
  action needs them. Synonym variation would weaken spatial clarity and bodily effort.
- Tears arrive after accumulated observation. The narrator's concealment of them becomes another
  relationship action, rather than a thematic explanation of filial love.
- This example does not establish a universal ban on interiority, memory, atmosphere, metaphor,
  long sentences, or ornate project voices. It demonstrates that chosen language should grow from
  viewpoint, action, pressure, and consequence.

## Draft and Review contrast pass

Before drafting, identify the concrete action, obstruction, object handling, silence, or choice that
can bear each intended emotional turn. Do not invent memory merely to increase sentiment; verify it
against approved history and give it a present trigger and consequence.

After drafting, compare the passage against all three examples:

1. Mark explanations, symmetrical exposition, balanced formulations, counted-time amplification,
   generic weather, sentimental montage, interchangeable similes, and thematic conclusions.
2. Ask which marked material changes perception, action, relationship pressure, or consequence.
   Remove or rebuild only what is detachable or redundantly interprets meaning already carried by
   the scene.
3. Check whether a reader can experience the emotional turn through selected evidence before being
   told what it means.
4. Preserve functional repetition, plain diction, deliberate reflection, project-grounded imagery,
   and genuine interior complication.
5. Recheck project-specific voice. Do not copy the father, railway, oranges, colors, syntax, or
   sentimental register into an unrelated Novel.
